# Date and trigger representation

## Accepted meanings

Use dates only to express actual appointments, hard deadlines, and deliberately chosen start/tickler triggers. A Waiting For follow-up date is a review trigger, distinct from the recipient's promised delivery date. A planning horizon or preferred finish is not automatically a task due date.

Preserve supplied dates and their type; do not silently normalize an ambiguous “Friday at ten” into a real calendar instant. Ask for the date, time, timezone, or AM/PM only when needed for an actual requested representation/edit. Mark unknown details without inventing them.

## Editing boundary

If the user says a Monday date was arbitrary, flag that mismatch and propose removing or clarifying its meaning while keeping the actual obligation visible. Do not infer that the obligation disappeared or select a replacement date. Missing follow-up is a gap to return to the person/GTD, not permission to choose one from convenience.

There are no default due dates, overdue thresholds, lead times, or automatic review intervals for new reminders. Express a reminder's accepted date accurately; decisions to negotiate deadlines, pick follow-ups, or schedule work belong outside this writing module.

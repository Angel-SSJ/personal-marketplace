# MoSCoW audits and correction

## Start with a selected method and bounded evidence

Audit a stated MoSCoW decision, outcome, and horizon. If a generic prioritization request has not selected this method, ask first. Inspect the relevant candidate scope, classification rationales, prerequisites, actual agreements, estimates, capacity, and uncertainty. Unavailable facts remain unknown; do not certify a whole backlog from a small sample.

## Diagnostic checks

| Symptom                                               | Evidence to inspect                                             | Minimum repair                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Every item is Must                                    | Omission consequences and viable workarounds                    | Challenge necessity and split coarse core/enhancement scope without demoting genuine obligations arbitrarily |
| A category has no timeframe                           | Intended outcome and relevant horizon                           | State the decision horizon or keep classification provisional                                                |
| A Must needs excluded/discretionary work              | Prerequisite chain and alternatives                             | Include the necessary prerequisite, find a viable alternative, or renegotiate the dependent outcome          |
| Many small Musts conceal large effort                 | Comparable effort estimates, shared prerequisites, coordination | Recalculate effort and actual capacity instead of counting items                                             |
| Good percentages hide an infeasible plan              | Total active effort, capacity, and uncertainty                  | Resolve the real shortfall; category balance is not feasibility proof                                        |
| No practical contingency                              | Could pool, meaningful flexibility, estimate risk               | Negotiate genuine optional scope without filler tasks or artificial quotas                                   |
| Won't disappears or returns silently                  | Agreed exclusion, owner/authority, horizon                      | Preserve the visible decision and renegotiate any reintroduction                                             |
| A promise disappears through relabeling               | Real accepted obligation and consent to change it               | Restore visibility and explicitly fulfill or renegotiate                                                     |
| Scope categories become daily order or calendar dates | Separate execution constraints and scheduling policy            | Return category meaning to scope and leave execution to the chosen workflow                                  |
| An old classification is reused despite changed facts | Changed horizon, dependency, agreement, estimate, or workaround | Reassess the affected decision and explain the change                                                        |

## Finding shape and severity

Give the observed evidence and inspected scope, violated category/feasibility distinction, practical consequence, smallest correction, and retest. Label inferences separately. Prioritize genuine obligation/safety/viability failures, then impossible dependencies or capacity, then recurrent ambiguity. Cosmetic label placement or an unusual percentage alone is not a defect.

Do not invent external legal/safety facts or claim domain correctness from a scope label. If a purported non-negotiable requirement cannot be verified, state the assumption and need for the appropriate evidence.

## Verify and prevent

Replay the decision: is the outcome still viable, are required prerequisites included, can estimated effort fit real capacity with uncertainty, and are proposed versus accepted exclusions clear? Review incomplete items at the relevant horizon boundary or material change. Check one recurring failure at its source; do not impose a heavy daily full-backlog audit.

Changing a label is not proof that an agreement changed or a task was completed. Report what was proposed, accepted, actually edited, and verified. No file naming/content checks or application-specific configuration are part of this audit.

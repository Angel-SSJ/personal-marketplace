## Minimum GTD routes

Nonactionable inputs become reference, Someday/Maybe, a genuine future reconsideration trigger (Tickler), or a discard proposal. An actionable finite multi-action outcome is a Project with a next action or real waiting relationship. Flexible executable actions go to Next Actions. External expected results go to Waiting For with responsible person, deliverable and follow-up. Real day/time obligations belong on the calendar. Do not invent dates, owners or acceptance of a suggested action. During clarification a genuinely quick, suitable authorized action may be done; Inbox processing must not become extended project execution.

These are reminder meanings, normally represented as lists/states and appropriate calendar/reminder mechanisms. They are not automatically tags, filenames or folders in 05_intake. A project plus its next action and support are related outputs, not mutually exclusive bins. A date-specific trigger alone does not make a project active under the user's support profile.

## Minimum PARA and CODE routes

Support is located by current usefulness: active finite project, ongoing Area standard/records, useful Resource interest, or inactive historical Archive. Project-exclusive sources use assets; editable work docs; scratches/superseded iterations drafts; frozen final snapshots submissions. A resource needs no task. Exploration can justify preserving material without a planned deliverable. Inspect existing knowledge and preserve sources/qualifiers; distill at use, not on every capture.

## Handoff

Pass actual goal, input identity, inspected evidence, candidate entity, known commitment, unresolved decision and authorized scope. Preserve accepted method outputs on return. Informational relations neither install nor load companions. When available, read the relevant skill and continue within authorization without asking a method-name question. When unavailable, use the local minimum for ordinary cases; mark deeper unresolved decisions rather than claim the companion ran. A handoff does not authorize a new commitment, entity, external communication or destructive operation.
